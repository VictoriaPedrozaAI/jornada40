# Retail Store Staffing Model — 80 FTEs / 3,200 h Cap

**Operating Schedule:** Monday–Sunday  
**Store Hours:** 09:00–22:00  
**FTE Baseline:** 40 h/week  
**Turn Length:** 8 h (7.5 h work + 0.5 h paid break)  
**Total Weekly Cap:** 3,200 h (80 FTEs × 40 h)  
**Legal Framework:** Ley Federal del Trabajo (LFT), Mexico

## 5. LFT Key Assumptions

| Assumption | Value | Legal Basis (LFT) |
|---|---|---|
| FTE baseline | 40 h/week | Art. 59 (phased to 2030) |
| Daily max (day shift) | 8 h | Art. 61 |
| Break | 30 min paid, part of workday | Art. 63 |
| Rest day | 1 rest day per 6 worked, with full pay | Art. 69 |
| Sunday work | 25% prima dominical | Art. 71 |
| Overtime | None assumed | Avoided to control cost |
| Electronic time tracking | Mandatory from 2027 | Art. 132 |

**Result:** The proposed model uses **3,190 h/week**, equivalent to **79.75 FTEs**, and remains within the **3,200 h cap**.
